YOUR BACKGROUND MUSIC GOES HERE
================================

Drop ONE music file into this folder named exactly:

    bgm.mp3

It plays on loop as the site's ambient BGM (the speaker button in the
bottom-right corner toggles it). MP3 is recommended; WAV and OGG also work.

To go back to the built-in generative music, just delete the file.

Notes:
- The music starts on your first click/keypress anywhere on the page
  (browsers block autoplay).
- The toggle preference is remembered per browser.
